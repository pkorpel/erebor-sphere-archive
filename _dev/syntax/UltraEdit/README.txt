These are macros and syntax highlight for UltraEdit (compatible with v12.00a).

Usage:
1. copy the wordfile into your UltraEdit installation folder (overwrite your
old file)
2. (optional) include the macro file ueditQuestsMacros

The second will give you a few pre-configured macros to create the quest gumps
faster. Go through the list of macros and assign your own shortcuts to them
for ease of use
